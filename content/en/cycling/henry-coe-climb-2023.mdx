## Ride Summary

**Henry Coe Climb** is the kind of ride with a short name and very honest legs. The public Strava record shows **51.7 mi / 3,858 ft / 4h45m**. The distance is shorter than Tierra Bella, but the climbing density is higher, so the day felt much harder.

The original Strava note was direct: Henry Coe is hard, and it had been a while since riding 50+ miles, so it was very tiring. That sentence is worth preserving because it sounds more like the day than any polished summary.

## Route

The public activity confirms this as a road climb around Henry Coe. The photos include a bridge, hillside views, a ranch-like stop, and the Visitor Center, so the route has a clear personality: not a flat mileage ride, but a day that slowly stacks up climbing.

## Logistics

## Notes

Henry Coe feels harder than the numbers suggest. It is not just about distance; every refill, snack, and climb becomes specific. If I repeat it, the main lesson is to plan it as a full climbing day and respect the fatigue that comes from 50+ miles plus elevation.
