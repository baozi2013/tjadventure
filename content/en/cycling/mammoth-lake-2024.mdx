## Ride Summary

The main riding goal of this Mammoth weekend was **Mammoth Gran Fondo**. After arriving through Yosemite and Tioga Pass the day before, we gathered at the start the next morning and finished the Strava-recorded **71.0 mi / 3,648 ft / 5h02m**.

The route sits at high altitude, so distance and climbing feel more significant than the raw numbers suggest. Fortunately, both condition and weather cooperated, and the final result felt steadier than expected.

## Route

This was a paved-road Gran Fondo starting from the Mammoth Lakes event area. The key was not the race label; it was pacing on high-altitude roads. Do not spend too much too early, or the later rollers become expensive.

## Logistics

- Trip context: the ride sat inside a four-day Mammoth family trip, with Day 1 arriving via Tioga Pass.
- Pacing: heart rate and perceived effort are more sensitive at elevation, so long-ride output needs early control.
- Recovery: after the ride, Convict Lake and the hot spring area work well for an easy walking day.
- Return: if driving back through Tioga Pass, check same-day access hours ahead of time.

## Notes

Mammoth Gran Fondo has a solid finish feeling: long paved distance, event atmosphere, and high-altitude setting all give it a clear memory tag. Eating with family after the ride was the most comfortable ending to the weekend.
