## Ride Summary

This was the first time **Mount Diablo** became a recorded cycling goal. The Strava title was `Diablo初体验`, and the public embed shows **43.1 mi / 4,386 ft / 4h09m**. The distance is not extreme, but more than four thousand feet of climbing makes it a full climbing day.

The public route map shows the ride approaching Mount Diablo from the **San Ramon / Danville** side. The Strava activity did not include additional photos, so the cover uses a publicly licensed summit photo. The route map and finish data are still the clearest record of the first Diablo ride.

From the Strava route map, the ride starts around San Ramon, heads north toward Danville, then enters the Mount Diablo climbing roads. The public activity does not provide exact parking, supply, or turnaround details, so this page only records verifiable route shape and completion data.
