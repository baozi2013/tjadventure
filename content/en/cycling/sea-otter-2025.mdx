## Ride Summary

Sea Otter was not just one route. It was a weekend mix of **riding, expo walking, and family activity**. Day 1 was the main ride, with Strava recording **52.2 mi / 3,392 ft / 3h31m**. Day 2 switched to a family ride.

The overall experience was excellent: strong event atmosphere, friendly route design, and easy ways for family to participate.

## Logistics

- Works well as a two-day, one-night plan: solo ride on Day 1, family ride on Day 2.
- The expo has a high brand density, so do not reserve time only for riding.
- The Monterey waterfront is a good easy ending after the event.

## Notes

Sea Otter is worth repeating. Its value is not one single scenic section, but the weekend cycling atmosphere: ride, walk the expo, and bring family into the event, all in one flow.
