# Camping Gear

This list covers the core family camping and car-camping gear we use most often right now. It starts with the reliable basics and will keep growing as the setup evolves.

## 1. The North Face Tent

- Use: Main overnight shelter with a balanced mix of wind protection and usable space.
- Experience: Roomy enough for four people, and reasonably quick to pitch.
- Extra space: The small vestibule area is useful for shoes, bags, and quick transitions.
- Note: For multi-day basecamp trips, we would add a tarp or shade shelter for more living space.

![The North Face tent](/gears/northfacetent.webp)

## 2. Tarp Groundsheet

- Use: A moisture barrier under the tent to keep damp ground and dirt away.
- Experience: Makes setup feel cleaner and packing up easier.
- Note: We mostly use this tarp as a groundsheet, not as the main shade canopy.

![Camping tarp](/gears/tarp.webp)

## 3. Exped Inflatable Sleeping Mat

- Use: Insulation from the ground plus better sleep comfort.
- Experience: Adds real support even when the ground is uneven.
- Note: For car camping, this is an easy choice over a thin traditional pad.

![Exped sleeping mat](/gears/expedmat.webp)

## 4. Mummy Sleeping Bag

- Use: Overnight warmth.
- Experience: Good wraparound warmth and a wider comfort range.
- Note: Worth bringing first for high-elevation trips or spring and fall camping.

![Mummy sleeping bag](/gears/mummybag.jpeg)

## Current Setup Summary

This setup is lightweight without feeling improvised:

1. The tent provides the sleeping space.
2. The tarp keeps the ground dry and cleaner.
3. The sleeping mat and bag protect the quality of overnight rest.

Stove, lighting, and storage notes will be added later.
