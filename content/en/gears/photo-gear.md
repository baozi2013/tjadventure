# Photo Gear

These are the two cameras we use most often on trips right now. The goal is simple: reliable images without carrying too much.

## 1. Sony a6300

- Role: Main travel camera.
- Strengths: Stable image quality, flexible lens options, and strong landscape and portrait results.
- Best for: Daytime scenery, shallow-depth portraits, and photos that benefit from more editing room.
- Note: It handles high-dynamic-range scenes like backlight and sunset better than a phone.

![Sony a6300](/gears/sonya6300.jpeg)

## 2. GoPro 10

- Role: Action and first-person recording camera.
- Strengths: Strong stabilization, small size, and lots of mounting options.
- Best for: Cycling, hiking, water activities, and recording movement.
- Note: It captures the feeling of the process and complements the main camera well.

![GoPro 10](/gears/gopro10.jpeg)

## Current Shooting Split

1. Main landscape photos: Sony a6300.
2. Action footage: GoPro 10.
3. Everyday snapshots: phones.

This combination is enough for most of our trips: good image quality without making the pack too heavy.
