## Overview

We went to Del Valle for a weekend outing, and the "Bay Area Little Switzerland" feeling was real. This trip was all about an easy walk, open lake views, and a relaxed family-friendly pace.

## Day 1 | Photos Tell The Story

We started around the **Arroyo Del Valle Staging Area**. The parking lot was already busy, and finding a spot was not easy.

![Del Valle entrance photo](/trips/del-valle-2026/day1-1.jpeg)

Once we reached the grassy slopes above the lake, the view opened up immediately: blue sky, green hills, and the reservoir below. The hike itself was not hard, and it worked well as a stop-and-photo kind of walk.

![Del Valle lake and grass hills](/trips/del-valle-2026/day1-2.jpeg)

The only awkward part was the parking enforcement. We ended up with a ticket, which snapped the day right back from vacation mode to real life.

## Takeaways

1. The scenery is beautiful, especially the hills and lake.
2. The hiking is friendly for a light weekend outing.
3. Plan parking carefully and read signs closely to avoid a ticket.
