## Overview

This was a short Tahoe weekend with **Fallen Leaf Lake** as the main base. It had the classic family camping rhythm: lake and water during the day, campground at night, then an easy return the next day.

## Day 1 | Into The Mountains, Then Camp

We started with a slow loop through the mountains. The snow peaks and meadow views were clean and open, and the weather changed quickly in a very Tahoe way.

![Day 1 valley, snow peaks, and meadow](/trips/fallen-leaf-2023/IMG_4087.jpg)

At Fallen Leaf Lake, the view opened up completely. The water color and clouds made the lake feel calm but still dramatic.

![Day 1 Fallen Leaf Lake panorama](/trips/fallen-leaf-2023/IMG_4095.jpg)

The kids got ice cream first, which was basically the official start of vacation mode.

![Day 1 ice cream break](/trips/fallen-leaf-2023/IMG_1573.jpeg)

We also stopped around the lodging and nearby buildings. The mountain cabin mood was strong.

![Day 1 cabin street view](/trips/fallen-leaf-2023/IMG_1584.jpeg)
![Day 1 wildflowers in the woods](/trips/fallen-leaf-2023/IMG_1598.jpeg)

By evening we were back at camp, with tents set up and a slow night ahead.

![Day 1 two tents at camp](/trips/fallen-leaf-2023/IMG_1599.jpeg)
![Day 1 lake sunset mood](/trips/fallen-leaf-2023/IMG_4105.jpg)

## Day 2 | Water, Sand, And An Easy Drive Home

The second day was all about family lake time: paddle board, water play, climbing on rocks, and kids with more energy than the adults.

![Day 2 playing on the lake](/trips/fallen-leaf-2023/IMG_1600.jpeg)

The beach took a lot of time too, including the classic bury-someone-in-sand project.

![Day 2 burying someone in the sand](/trips/fallen-leaf-2023/IMG_4115.jpg)

We added a few more shoreline photos. Sunny Tahoe is very easy to photograph.

![Day 2 lake shore view](/trips/fallen-leaf-2023/IMG_1602.jpeg)

On the way home, the kids even joined the car wash and supply stop, which made the ending feel participatory too.

![Day 2 car wash stop 1](/trips/fallen-leaf-2023/IMG_1652.jpeg)
![Day 2 car wash stop 2](/trips/fallen-leaf-2023/IMG_1655.jpeg)

## Takeaways

1. Fallen Leaf Lake works very well for a family camping weekend.
2. Lake and mountain views are consistently strong.
3. Water play plus camping is an easy win for kids, and the drive home does not have to feel rushed.
