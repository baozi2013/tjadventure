## Overview

This four-day trip had a big contrast in mood. The first half was **Miami**, focused on street art, beaches, and slow city walking. The second half moved to **Houston** for space exhibits and large rockets.

If you like a trip that combines city atmosphere with a themed experience, this route works well for a weekend plus one or two days off.

## Day 1 | Wynwood: Murals, Color Blocks, And High Saturation

The first day was mainly around **Wynwood**. The fun here is not one single stop, but walking block by block as different mural styles keep appearing.

![Wynwood Walls street mural](/trips/miami-houston-2026/day1-1.jpg)
![Street scene around JOEY'S WYNWOOD](/trips/miami-houston-2026/day1-2.jpg)
![Color blocks outside Miami Mojito Company](/trips/miami-houston-2026/day1-3.jpg)
![Pink mural wall in Wynwood](/optimized/trips/miami-houston-2026/day1-4.webp)

This area is best in daylight, when the colors are stronger. Plain or light-colored clothing works better than busy patterns against the murals.

## Day 2 | Miami Beach: 10th Street Lifeguard Tower And A Slow Beach Walk

The second day moved to **Miami Beach**. The beach itself was relaxing, but the row of colorful lifeguard towers was the most memorable visual detail. The 10th Street tower was especially photogenic.

![Miami Beach 10th Street lifeguard tower](/trips/miami-houston-2026/day2-1.jpg)
![South Beach casual beach photo](/trips/miami-houston-2026/day2-2.jpg)
![Palm-lined seaside walk](/trips/miami-houston-2026/day2-3.jpg)
![Meal at Puerto Sagua](/trips/miami-houston-2026/day2-4.jpg)

Dinner at **Puerto Sagua** fit the beach-city rhythm: walk and photograph by the water during the day, then return to town for food in the evening.

## Day 3 | Transition Day: Night View And Light Shopping

The third day was more of a transition and reset day. We kept it light with city night views and a little shopping, which preserved energy for the final day.

![City night view and easy transition day](/trips/miami-houston-2026/day3-1.jpg)

For a multi-city photo-and-walk trip, leaving half a day as a buffer feels much better than filling every day with sights.

## Day 4 | Houston: From Space Station Exhibits To Large Rockets

The last day was all about the space-themed exhibits in **Houston**. It felt completely different from Miami: more industrial, more technical, and more focused on science storytelling.

![International Space Station exhibit area](/trips/miami-houston-2026/day4-1.jpg)
![Outdoor rocket exhibit](/trips/miami-houston-2026/day4-2.jpg)
![Large rocket close-up](/trips/miami-houston-2026/day4-3.jpg)
![Photo stop inside the rocket exhibit hall](/trips/miami-houston-2026/day4-4.jpg)

The rockets feel bigger in person than in photos. I would reserve at least half a day and avoid compressing this stop too much.

## Takeaways

1. The value of Miami plus Houston is the style switch: street art and beach first, space and industry after.
2. Miami is better for slow walking and photos; Houston is better for focused exhibit time.
3. A light transition day makes the four-day route feel much easier.
4. If repeating it, I would lock in Miami Beach and Wynwood first, then use the Houston space area as the closing highlight.
