## Overview

This Christmas trip was deliberately easy: walk around **Monterey Fisherman's Wharf**, then drive into **17-Mile Drive** for ocean views and coastal stops.

Overall, it was a light walk plus scenic drive route. Not tiring, but very photogenic.

## Fisherman's Wharf | Full Holiday Mood

We started at the wharf. There were plenty of holiday decorations, and the whole place felt festive. A coastal town plus Christmas decor makes vacation-style photos very easy.

![Christmas decorations at Monterey Wharf](/optimized/trips/monterey-17miles-2024/day1-1.webp)
![Casual street photo around the wharf](/trips/monterey-17miles-2024/day1-2.jpg)
![Family photo near the waterfront](/trips/monterey-17miles-2024/day1-3.jpg)

## 17-Mile Drive | Beautiful Coast, Many Mansions

The second half was 17-Mile Drive. It is basically a "walk a bit, reach another viewpoint" kind of route. Rocks, waves, and sky layers were all beautiful, especially around sunset.

![Small coastal scene on 17-Mile Drive](/trips/monterey-17miles-2024/day1-5.jpg)
![17-Mile Drive coastline at sunset](/trips/monterey-17miles-2024/day1-6.jpg)
![17-Mile Drive rocky coast](/trips/monterey-17miles-2024/day1-7.jpg)

We also wandered around Carmel for a bit. The street scenes and small shops had a good mood.

![Carmel shop at night](/optimized/trips/monterey-17miles-2024/day1-4.webp)
![Carmel courtyard street photo](/trips/monterey-17miles-2024/day1-8.jpeg)

The most direct feeling while driving this route: the scenery is beautiful, the houses are huge, and it is hard not to feel a little envious.

## Takeaways

1. Christmas is a good time for an easy family Monterey plus 17-Mile Drive outing.
2. Fisherman's Wharf has strong holiday atmosphere and easy photos.
3. Leave some time around sunset for 17-Mile Drive; the light is much better then.
