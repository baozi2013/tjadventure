## Overview

This was a one-day family outing in downtown Nanjing, centered on **Hongshan Forest Zoo**, with an evening walk at nearby **Mochou Lake Park** to close out the day.

Hongshan Forest Zoo is the kind of place you can spend a full day at — it has a wide range of animals, and most enclosures have been redesigned to feel more naturalistic, which makes it noticeably more pleasant than a traditional zoo. We kept a relaxed pace throughout, with a snack break in the middle, which worked well with kids in tow.

## Hongshan Zoo | From Hippos And Orangutans To Flamingos And Tigers

We took a photo at the entrance plaza first — the wooden gate and the topiary animal sculptures at Hongshan Forest Zoo are distinctive and worth capturing before heading in.

![Zoo entrance plaza](/trips/nanjing-hongshan-zoo-2026/day1-1.jpg)

The two kids also posed next to a giant gorilla sculpture by the gate, which felt like the official kickoff for the day.
![Photo with the gorilla sculpture](/trips/nanjing-hongshan-zoo-2026/day1-2.jpg)

The hippo enclosure left a strong first impression — it lay motionless by the pool, looking even bigger than expected.
![Hippo](/trips/nanjing-hongshan-zoo-2026/day1-3.jpg)

The orangutan area is an open-air outdoor space, and through the glass we could see two orangutans foraging casually on the grass, quite close to the viewing area.
![Orangutans foraging](/trips/nanjing-hongshan-zoo-2026/day1-4.jpg)

In the reptile section, an iguana sat motionless on a branch, its coloring and texture striking up close.
![Iguana](/trips/nanjing-hongshan-zoo-2026/day1-5.jpg)

The flamingo area was the most colorful stop of the day — a flock gathered under bamboo fencing and palm leaves, their orange-pink feathers making for great photos.
![Flock of flamingos](/trips/nanjing-hongshan-zoo-2026/day1-6.jpg)

The Asian elephant enclosure sits next to an older building, with the herd slowly moving behind the fence, their size quite imposing up close.
![Asian elephants](/trips/nanjing-hongshan-zoo-2026/day1-7.jpg)

The tiger enclosure is viewed through mesh netting — the tiger paced slowly through the trees, and even from a distance it had a strong presence.
![Tiger](/trips/nanjing-hongshan-zoo-2026/day1-8.jpg)

We also passed a meerkat area along the way, with three meerkats darting around tree stumps and log piles, full of energy.
![Meerkats](/trips/nanjing-hongshan-zoo-2026/day1-9.jpg)

Partway through, we grabbed a snack inside the park — sweet potato fries and fried chicken bites — and rested on a rock before continuing.
![Snack break inside the zoo](/trips/nanjing-hongshan-zoo-2026/day1-10.jpg)

## Mochou Lake Park | An Evening Wind-Down

It was still light out when we left the zoo, so we made a quick stop at nearby **Mochou Lake Park**. The gate was hung with red lanterns, and the two kids posed for one more photo there, closing out the day.

![Photo at the Mochou Lake Park gate](/trips/nanjing-hongshan-zoo-2026/day1-11.jpg)

## Takeaways

1. Hongshan Forest Zoo has a wide range of animals and many naturalistic enclosures — plan for a full, unhurried day.
2. The hippo, orangutan, flamingo, elephant, tiger, and meerkat areas are all worth lingering at, and they photograph well too.
3. Building in a snack break midway helps keep kids' energy and mood up for the second half.
4. If you still have energy after the zoo, a walk at nearby Mochou Lake Park makes for an easy, relaxed way to close the day.
