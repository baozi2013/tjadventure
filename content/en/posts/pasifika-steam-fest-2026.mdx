The main activity this time was **Pasifika STEAM Fest**, focused on hands-on experiences and interactive photo stops. The photos outside the Roblox building were just a quick ending, not the core of the day.

## Overview

From the photo timestamps, the core window was roughly **1:33 PM to 3:12 PM (PDT)**. The flow worked like this:

1. Check in, then start with indoor activity stations such as Build It Up and themed installations.
2. Add family photos and photo-strip shots inside the event area.
3. After the event, stop briefly outside for a few closing photos.

## Pasifika STEAM Fest Indoor Interactive Area

The first impression was a large interactive setup. Once the kids got their event lanyards, they quickly entered activity mode. The format felt more like hands-on stations than passive display boards.

## Transportation And Parking

For this kind of campus event, arrival planning matters:

1. Arrive 15-20 minutes early so check-in and security feel calm.
2. The event venue parking lot was **$18 per car** this time.
3. We actually parked near **Roblox**, where parking was free for 2 hours, then walked to the event center.
4. Since the route switches between indoor and outdoor spaces, light athletic shoes are easiest for kids.

## Cost And Supplies

Event cost and registration rules depend on the organizer for that year. Bring water and a simple snack so kids do not fade while waiting in line.

## Takeaways

- STEAM events like this are very good half-day family outings: controlled time, high participation density.
- Do the indoor interactive area first, then use the later part for photos.
- The outdoor closing photos were a small add-on; the core experience was still Pasifika STEAM Fest itself.
- If kids like building, challenge stations, or tech themes, this is worth repeating.
