## Overview

This was a weekend day trip to **Pinnacles National Park**. The pace was easy, and the post is mostly a photo-walk record.

The day roughly followed this route:

1. Entrance sign photo
2. Rock-wall trail and viewpoints
3. Cave area exploring
4. Lake finish with a family photo

## Day 1 | Photos Tell The Story

We started with a family photo by the entrance stone wall and park sign, the standard "we made it" opening.

![Pinnacles entrance photo](/trips/pinnicle-2025/day1-1.jpeg)

Inside the park, the trail quickly moved through typical rock-wall terrain. Some sections were close to cliffs and railings, with open views that made couple photos feel more adventurous.

![Group photo at a rock-wall viewpoint](/trips/pinnicle-2025/day1-2.jpeg)

Then we reached the cave entrance. The rock texture and light were layered and very photogenic.

![Cave entrance group photo](/trips/pinnicle-2025/day1-3.jpeg)
![Light and shadow inside the cave](/trips/pinnicle-2025/day1-4.JPG)
![Boulder photo spot deeper in the cave](/trips/pinnicle-2025/day1-5.JPG)

We ended by the lake. The sunlight and water were both calm, and one final family photo made the day feel complete.

![Family photo by the lake](/trips/pinnicle-2025/day1-6.jpeg)

## Takeaways

1. Pinnacles works well as a flexible day trip.
2. Rock walls plus caves give the photos a lot of variety.
3. It is kid-friendly if you choose the right route and keep the pace controlled.

Next time, with more time, I would add another loop and revisit it properly.
