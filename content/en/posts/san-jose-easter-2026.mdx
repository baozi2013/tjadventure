This Easter event was a classic half-day family outing: enough activity for the kids, easy for adults to follow and take photos, and not rushed.

## Overview

From the photo timestamps, the core event time was about **10:44 AM to 12:09 PM (PDT)**. The main flow fit within two hours:

1. Check in, then go to the themed tent area for Easter Bunny photos.
2. Let the kids warm up at the playground and grass area.
3. Gather for staff instructions, then enter the grass area for the egg hunt and mini games.
4. Return to the theme area for final photos before heading home.

## Transportation And Gear

If you are planning a similar community Easter event:

1. Arrive 20-30 minutes early for easier parking and check-in.
2. Bring a small basket or bag so you are not scrambling for a container on site.
3. Grass events have strong sun exposure, so prepare hats, sunscreen, and water.
4. If you want Easter Bunny photos, take them early before the line gets longer.

## Cost And Supplies

These community events are often free or low-cost, but registration depends on the organizer each year. Feed the kids a small meal before the event and focus on water during the activity.

## Takeaways

- The best rhythm was: burn energy first, then line up, then egg hunt.
- The themed photo area was complete enough to deserve 10-15 dedicated minutes.
- Grass games were easy to join and good for family interaction.
- For Bay Area families, this is a low-prep, high-happiness weekend activity.
