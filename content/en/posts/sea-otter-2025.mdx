## Overview

This time we joined the **Monterey Sea Otter** bike expo and rides over two days and one night:

- Day 1: 50-mile ride, finished comfortably
- Day 2: family 10+ mile fun ride
- The rest of the time was walking the expo and checking out brands

The event mood was excellent, and both the routes and family activities felt friendly.

## Day 1 | Easy Finish On The 50-Mile Ride

On the first day, I started with the riding group. Everyone felt good, and the weather helped. The 50-mile route was easier than expected once the pace settled.

## Around The Expo

The Sea Otter expo area is genuinely large. Brands, bikes, parts, gear, and clothing are everywhere, and it is easy to spend a lot of time just walking and talking to booths.

## Takeaways

1. Sea Otter works well as a combined cycling and family activity weekend.
2. A distance challenge on Day 1 plus a family fun ride on Day 2 felt balanced.
3. The expo has a high brand density, so leave enough time to walk it.

Two days and one night felt very full. If the schedule works, I would come back next year.
