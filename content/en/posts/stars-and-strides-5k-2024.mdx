## Overview

This was a relaxed **Stars and Strides 5K** event day. The rhythm was friendly, with enough race atmosphere while still feeling suitable for family participation.

The day was simple:

1. Arrive in the morning for check-in and warm-up.
2. Enter the course and finish the 5K at our own pace.
3. Take finish-line photos and keep the memory.

## Before The Race | Warm Up First

We arrived fairly early, and plenty of runners were already on site. Volunteers and zones were clear. Warming up first made the run feel much better.

![Pre-race arrival and event mood](/trips/stars-and-strides-5k-2024/day1-1.jpg)

## On The Course | Run Your Own Pace

We were not chasing pace this time. The goal was to finish steadily. The course had many families and friend groups, and the atmosphere felt encouraging rather than pressuring.

## Takeaways

1. A 5K like this is great for families or friends: low barrier, high participation.
2. Running steadily at your own pace feels better than blindly chasing time.
3. For good photos, agree on shooting spots ahead of time: start, middle course, and finish.
4. As a half-day weekend activity, the exercise and ceremony were just right.
