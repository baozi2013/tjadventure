## Overview

This was a one-day **Stars and Strides 5K-10K Run** in downtown San Jose. Three of us signed up together, gathered near the start for photos in the morning, ran the course, then picked up medals and had a celebratory drink at the finish.

The event was sponsored by Bloom Energy and Tarana, with proceeds benefiting the Valley Health Foundation, so it carried a bit of a charity feel alongside the running.

## Before The Race | Warming Up Under Downtown High-Rises

When we arrived, the start area was already lively, with palm trees and office towers giving the whole street a summer feel. We grouped up for a photo first, wearing bibs 590, 591, and 776 in matching black Bloom Energy / Tarana shirts.

![Group photo at the start](/trips/stars-and-stripes-5k-2026/day1-1.jpg)

During the warm-up we grabbed a few more shots in the crowd, with the tents and aid station already set up near the start.
![In the crowd before the race](/trips/stars-and-stripes-5k-2026/day1-2.jpg)

Right before the start, we checked in with each other in line, feeling pretty relaxed about it.
![Group photo before the start](/trips/stars-and-stripes-5k-2026/day1-3.jpg)

## On The Course | A Joyful Push Near I-280

Once on the course, the pack moved through downtown streets and into a residential stretch, with plenty of runners in holiday-themed outfits keeping the mood festive.

![Crowd near the start of the course](/trips/stars-and-stripes-5k-2026/day1-4.jpg)

Passing the I-280 North sign in the middle of the course, we raised hands together for a celebratory moment — the most energetic shot of the day.
![Hands raised near the mid-course](/trips/stars-and-stripes-5k-2026/day1-5.jpg)

## Finish Area | Medals And A Toast

After crossing the line, we picked up our finisher medals at the finish area, both feeling a strong sense of accomplishment and smiling wide.

![Finisher medal photo](/trips/stars-and-stripes-5k-2026/day1-6.jpg)

After the medals, we had a celebratory drink under a tent on the grass — that kind of slow-down time after a run is always the best part.
![Celebratory drink at the finish](/trips/stars-and-stripes-5k-2026/day1-7.jpg)

## Takeaways

1. The Stars and Strides 5K-10K is great for running with friends, with a friendly course and a charity angle that adds to the sense of participation.
2. The downtown route has good scenery, alternating between office towers, palm trees, and residential streets, so the run never feels boring.
3. Arriving early for a group photo and leaving time to celebrate at the finish are the two things most worth planning for events like this.
