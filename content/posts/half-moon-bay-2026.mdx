这次 Half Moon Bay 是一个很轻的七月半日行程：下午先在海边高处看沙滩上的海豹和海鸟，再走进一段海岸柏树林，最后在紫色和白色的海边花田里等雾慢慢散开。

This Half Moon Bay outing was an easy July half-day on the coast: first watching seals and seabirds from a bluff, then stepping into a coastal cypress grove, and finally waiting for the fog to open over purple and white seaside flowers.

## 行程概览 / Trip Overview

相册时间显示，核心拍照大约从 **13:12 到 17:48（PDT）**。这不是赶景点的路线，更像是沿着 Half Moon Bay 一带的海岸慢慢找几个画面：野生动物、柏树、海雾、花田和山脊。

The album timestamps put the main photo window around **1:12 PM to 5:48 PM (PDT)**. This was not a checklist-style route; it was more about slowly collecting a few coastal scenes around Half Moon Bay: wildlife, cypress trees, fog, flowers, and ridgelines.

- 路线 / Route: 海边高处看海豹 -> 柏树林短走 -> 海岸花田拍照。
- 节奏 / Pace: 半日自驾 + 轻散步，体力要求不高。
- 天气 / Weather: 海边雾和风都明显，太阳一出来体感又会变暖。
- 适合 / Best for: 想轻松看海、拍照、顺路散步的人。

![Half Moon Bay 海边沙滩上的海豹和海鸟 / seals and seabirds on a Half Moon Bay area beach](/trips/half-moon-bay-2026/day1-01.jpg)

## Day 1｜海豹沙滩与悬崖风 / Seal Overlook and Bluff Wind

第一段最有记忆点的是从高处往下看沙滩。海豹躺在靠近浪线的沙地上，旁边是一大群海鸟；远处浪一直往岸上推，画面很安静，但风和海声应该都不小。

The first memorable stretch was the view down onto the beach. Seals were resting near the surf line, with a large group of seabirds nearby; the scene looked quiet from above, though the wind and ocean noise were probably strong in person.

![海豹在浪线旁休息 / seals resting near the surf line](/trips/half-moon-bay-2026/day1-02.jpg)

这种点最适合停在高处看，不适合下去打扰动物。拍照也不用追求离得很近，保留一点海浪、沙滩和悬崖边的距离，反而更像北加州海岸。

This kind of stop is best enjoyed from the bluff, without trying to get close to the animals. The photos work better with some distance anyway: surf, sand, and cliff edge all in the same frame feel very Northern California.

![站在高处看沙滩上的海豹 / looking down at seals from the bluff](/trips/half-moon-bay-2026/day1-04.jpg)

## Day 1｜柏树林短走 / A Short Walk Through Cypress Trees

看完海边之后，路线切进一段柏树林。这里的气氛和沙滩完全不一样：地面干燥，树干很密，枝条横向伸出来，走进去会有一种天然隧道的感觉。

After the ocean overlook, the route shifted into a cypress grove. The mood changed completely: dry ground, dense trunks, and branches reaching sideways, giving the path a natural tunnel feeling.

![Half Moon Bay 海岸附近的柏树林小路 / cypress grove path near the Half Moon Bay coast](/trips/half-moon-bay-2026/day1-06.jpg)

这一段不需要走很久，重点是换一个节奏。前面是开阔海面，后面是低云和花田，中间插一段柏树林，整趟半日线就不会只有“看海”一个画面。

This section does not need to be long. Its value is the change of rhythm: open ocean first, fog and flowers later, and this cypress grove in between so the whole half-day is more than just one coastal view.

![阴天海面和低云 / gray ocean and low clouds](/trips/half-moon-bay-2026/day1-07.jpg)

## Day 1｜雾散后的海边花田 / Coastal Flowers After the Fog Opened

下午后段最出片的是海边的紫白花田。照片里一开始还有低云压在海面上，后来山脊和蓝天慢慢露出来，紫色 agapanthus、白色花球、远处的海和山刚好叠在一起。

The most photogenic part came later in the afternoon, in the purple and white coastal flowers. The early frames still had low cloud over the water, but the ridgeline and blue sky gradually opened up, layering purple agapanthus, white blooms, ocean, and hills together.

![海边紫白花田和低云 / purple and white coastal flowers under low cloud](/trips/half-moon-bay-2026/day2-02.jpg)

这一段拍照的关键不是找一个“标准机位”，而是根据雾的变化换方向。朝海面拍会更柔和，朝山拍会更清爽；雾刚散开的时候，天空和山的颜色会比中午硬光更舒服。

The trick here was not finding one standard photo spot, but changing direction with the fog. Facing the ocean gave softer frames; facing the hills made the scene cleaner and brighter. When the fog had just opened, the sky and ridge colors felt gentler than harsh midday light.

![花田、山脊和刚散开的雾 / flowers, ridgeline, and lifting fog](/trips/half-moon-bay-2026/day2-04.jpg)

![紫色 agapanthus 和海岸山地 / purple agapanthus with coastal hills](/trips/half-moon-bay-2026/day2-05.jpg)

花田里最容易拍好的方式，是让人站在花的边缘或已有小路上，用花做前景，不需要真的踩进很深的位置。这样画面有层次，也更不容易破坏植物。

The easiest way to photograph the flower field is to keep the person near the edge or on existing paths, using the flowers as foreground instead of stepping deep into them. The photo gets more depth, and the plants are better protected.

![海边花田里的人像 / portrait among coastal flowers](/trips/half-moon-bay-2026/day2-08.jpg)

![雾气里的海岸花田 / coastal flowers in fog](/trips/half-moon-bay-2026/day2-09.jpg)

## 交通与安全 / Logistics and Safety

这条半日线适合自驾串起来。Half Moon Bay 一带海岸点之间距离不算远，但周末或节日前后停车会比较随机，看到合适的车位就不要太贪心。

This half-day route works best by car. The coastal stops around Half Moon Bay are not far apart, but parking can be unpredictable on weekends or around holidays, so it is worth taking a good spot when one appears.

- 衣服 / Layers: 七月也要带外套；海边低云、风和太阳会不断切换。
- 鞋子 / Shoes: 悬崖边、柏树林和花田边缘都适合穿好走的鞋。
- 野生动物 / Wildlife: 看海豹和海鸟时保持距离，留在安全观景位置。
- 拍照 / Photos: 花田适合用前景，不要踩进没有路径的植物区。
- 时间 / Timing: 下午后段如果雾散开，山、花和海的颜色会更有层次。

The practical rule is simple: bring layers, walk carefully near bluff edges, keep distance from wildlife, and let the fog be part of the plan instead of fighting it.

实际准备可以很简单：带外套，悬崖边慢一点，和野生动物保持距离，把海雾当成行程的一部分，而不是一个需要避开的麻烦。

## 小结 / Takeaways

1. Half Moon Bay 很适合做半日轻行程，不用排满，几个海岸点串起来就够丰富。 / Half Moon Bay works well as an easy half-day outing; a few coastal stops are enough.
2. 海豹、海鸟和浪线是这趟的自然开场，适合远远看、安静拍。 / The seals, seabirds, and surf line made a natural opening, best watched quietly from a distance.
3. 柏树林给路线换了一个节奏，短走就很有记忆点。 / The cypress grove added a different rhythm, even with only a short walk.
4. 花田最吃天气变化，雾散和蓝天之间的过渡反而最适合拍照。 / The flower field depended on changing weather; the transition between fog and blue sky was the best photo window.
5. 七月海边不要只按夏天准备，防风外套和好走的鞋比想象中重要。 / Even in July, do not dress only for summer; a wind layer and comfortable shoes matter more than expected.

这次最喜欢的画面，是紫色花、白色花、灰色海雾和突然露出来的蓝天同时出现在一张照片里。Half Moon Bay 的好看，很多时候就藏在这种天气切换里。

My favorite frame from this trip has purple flowers, white flowers, gray ocean fog, and a sudden patch of blue sky all at once. That is often where Half Moon Bay looks best: in the middle of changing weather.
